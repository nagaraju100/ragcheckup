# Gift Cards

## About Northwind Gear Gift Cards

Gift cards make the perfect present for any outdoor enthusiast. They can be used at any Northwind Gear retail store or on northwindgear.com.

## Purchase & Redemption

Gift cards are available in denominations from $25 to $500. Digital gift cards are delivered by email within 30 minutes of purchase. Physical gift cards arrive by mail within 5–7 business days.

To redeem:
1. Present your gift card at checkout (physical or digital)
2. For online orders, enter the gift card code at checkout
3. Partial balances remain on the gift card for future use

## Gift Card Terms

- Gift cards are issued by Northwind Gear Inc.
- No expiration date and no fees
- If the purchase price exceeds the gift card balance, the remaining amount must be paid by another method
- Gift cards cannot be redeemed for cash except as required by law
- Lost or stolen gift cards cannot be replaced
- Gift cards cannot be returned or exchanged
- Northwind Gear is not responsible for gift cards that are lost, stolen, damaged, or used without authorization

## Corporate & Bulk Orders

For corporate gifts of 10 or more cards, contact corporate-gifts@northwindgear.com for custom printing, bulk discounts (5% off for 25+ cards), and dedicated account management.
