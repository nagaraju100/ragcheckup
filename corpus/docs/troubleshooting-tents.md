# Troubleshooting — Tents

## Setup Issues

### Tent Pole Problems

| Problem | Likely Cause | Solution |
|---|---|---|
| Pole clips won't snap onto fabric | Pole diameter mismatch | Verify pole size matches tent model (DAC featherlite for 2A/2B, Easton for 4A/4B) |
| Pole section won't connect | Damaged ferrule or hub | Replace damaged section (PN: NW-RT-POL) |
| Spring steel pole snapped | Cold temperature flexing | Do not flex poles below -10°C. Store poles in a pole sleeve |

### Rainfly Issues

| Problem | Likely Cause | Solution |
|---|---|---|
| Rainfly flaps excessively | Guinea straps not adjusted | Adjust all guinea straps for tension. Ensure ridgeline is tight |
| Condensation drips inside | Poor ventilation | Open roof vents and door vents. Reduce inner tent contact with fly |
| Zipper sticks | Sand or debris in teeth | Clean zipper with fresh water. Apply zipper lubricant (PN: NW-ZIP-LUB) |

## Common Repair Scenarios

### Floor Leak

1. Identify the leak location (inside the tent at night with a flashlight)
2. Clean the area with rubbing alcohol and let dry
3. Apply seam sealer or repair patch (PN: NW-RT-FLOOR)
4. Allow 24 hours to cure before reassembly

### Fabric Tear

Small tears (<5cm) can be repaired with the included repair patches (PN: NW-RT-PATCH). For larger tears, use the sewing kit (PN: NW-RT-SWK) with ripstop nylon matching fabric color.

### Buckle Failure

Broken buckles are covered under warranty for the first 2 years. Out of warranty, replacement buckles can be ordered:
- Side-release buckle (small): PN: NW-RT-BUC-S
- Side-release buckle (large): PN: NW-RT-BUC-L
- Cam buckle: PN: NW-RT-CAM

## Ventilation Guide

Proper ventilation prevents condensation buildup:
- Open all available vents
- Position tent with door away from wind
- Ensure rainfly overhangs all sides
- Leave ground vents open (if equipped)
- Avoid drying clothes inside the tent

## Snow Loading

In snowy conditions:
- Use snow stakes (PN: NW-RT-SP) instead of standard stakes
- Dig out vestibule to reduce wind loading
- Brush snow off the rainfly regularly
- Never exceed the tent's rated snow load (see model specifications)

## Storage

After use, always:
1. Set up the tent to dry completely
2. Clean and dry all components
3. Store in a dry, ventilated location
4. Do NOT store in the packed stuff sack long-term
5. Check for wear and tear before next trip
