# Troubleshooting — Stoves

## Common Issues and Error Codes

### Canister Stoves (CS1, CS2)

| Symptom | Error Code | Likely Cause | Solution |
|---|---|---|---|
| Flame keeps flickering | ERR-FL-01 | Canister not connected properly | Unscrew and reconnect firmly |
| Weak flame at high altitude | ERR-FL-02 | Low canister pressure | Preheat canister, use inverted position |
| No spark from igniter | ERR-IGN-01 | Piezo igniter disconnected | Check connection to burner base |
| Spark but no ignition | ERR-IGN-02 | Worn igniter tip | Replace igniter (PN: NW-ST-IGN) |
| Flame extinguished by wind | N/A | No windscreen attached | Attach integrated or external windscreen |

### Liquid Fuel Stoves (LF1, LF2)

| Symptom | Error Code | Likely Cause | Solution |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fuel does not flow | ERR-FL-03 | Clogged fuel valve | Clean valve assembly with PN: NW-ST-JET |
| Pressure loss during use | ERR-FL-04 | Worn seal ring | Replace seal kit (PN: NW-ST-SEAL) |
| Leak at hose connection | ERR-FL-05 | Damaged hose gasket | Replace hose (PN: NW-ST-HS) |
| Flame too yellow | N/A | Insufficient air intake | Clean burner ports, check ventilation |
| Difficult to prime | N/A | Worn primer cup | Replace primer cup (included in seal kit) |

### Integrated Systems (IS1, IS2)

| Symptom | Error Code | Likely Cause | Solution |
|---|---|---|---|
| Stove does not click to pot | ERR-IS-01 | Pot mounting clips worn | Replace clips (PN: NW-IS1-CL) |
| Uneven heat distribution | N/A | Clogged burner holes | Clean burner with needle (included in kit) |
| Gas canister loose | ERR-IS-03 | Canister adapter worn | Replace adapter (PN: NW-IS1-AD) |

## Regular Maintenance

After each season, perform the following maintenance:
1. Clean the burner ports with a needle or thin wire
2. Inspect fuel lines for cracks or wear
3. Replace seal kits annually for liquid fuel stoves
4. Store canister stopes in a dry location
5. Test all components before your next trip
