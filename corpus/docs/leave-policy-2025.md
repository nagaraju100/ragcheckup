# Leave & Time-Off Policy

**Effective Date:** 2025-01-15
**Version:** 2025.1

## Overview

Northwind Gear provides a competitive leave package to support employee well-being and work-life balance. This policy applies to all full-time and part-time employees. All employees accrue leave at their prorated rate from their start date.

## Paid Time Off (PTO)

### Accrual Rates

| Years of Service | Annual PTO Hours | Monthly Accrual | Carryover Limit |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–1 years | 150 hours | 12.5 hours | 50 hours |
| 2–4 years | 200 hours | 16.7 hours | 75 hours |
| 5–9 years | 260 hours | 21.7 hours | 100 hours |
| 10+ years | 320 hours | 26.7 hours | No limit |

Employees begin accruing PTO on their start date. New hires may use up to **20 hours** of PTO after completing 60 days of employment (reduced from 90 days). Unused PTO rolls over to the next calendar year up to the carryover limit.

## Mental Health Days

Employees are entitled to **5 additional paid mental health days** per year beyond their standard PTO allocation. These days may be used without medical documentation and do not require advance notice. Mental health days cannot be combined with other leave types.

### Mental Health Resources

Northwind Gear provides free access to:
- Confidential counselling through our Employee Assistance Program (EAP)
- Subscription to mindfulness and meditation apps
- Quarterly wellness workshops (optional attendance)

## Sick Leave

Employees accrue **60 hours** of sick leave per year regardless of tenure (increased from 40 hours). Sick leave may be used for personal illness, medical appointments, or to care for a family member. Documentation is now required only for absences exceeding 5 consecutive days (increased from 3).

### Sick Leave Usage

Sick leave requests should be submitted via the HR portal at least 24 hours in advance for planned appointments. Emergency sick leave may be reported by phone on the first day of absence.

## Paid Holidays

Northwind Gear observes **11 paid holidays** annually:
- New Year's Day (January 1)
- Martin Luther King Jr. Day (third Monday in January)
- Presidents' Day (third Monday in February)
- Memorial Day (last Monday in May)
- Independence Day (July 4)
- Juneteenth (June 19)
- Labor Day (first Monday in September)
- Thanksgiving (fourth Thursday in November)
- Day after Thanksgiving
- Christmas Eve (half-day)
- Christmas Day (December 25)

Holiday employees who work on a scheduled holiday receive **time-and-a-half** pay. Retail store employees scheduled to work on a holiday receive **double time** plus an additional floating holiday.

## Parental Leave

### Maternity Leave
Full-time female employees are entitled to **16 weeks** of paid maternity leave following childbirth. The leave must be taken within the first 12 months after the birth or adoption. A medical certificate is required for leave exceeding 8 weeks (increased from 4).

### Paternal Leave
All full-time employees are entitled to **8 weeks** of paid paternal leave following the birth or adoption of a child (increased from 4 weeks). This leave must be taken within 12 months of the child's placement (extended from 6 months).

### Adoption Leave
Employees adopting a child through a licensed agency are entitled to **12 weeks** of paid leave (increased from 8 weeks). The leave must begin within 60 days of the child's placement (extended from 30 days).

## Unpaid Leave

Employees with at least 6 months of service may request up to **12 weeks** of unpaid leave for medical, family, or personal reasons (extended from 8 weeks). Requests must be submitted in writing at least 21 days in advance, except for emergency situations.
