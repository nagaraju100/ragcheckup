# Dress Code Policy

**Effective Date:** 2024-01-15
**Version:** 2024.1

## General Guidelines

Northwind Gear maintains a business casual dress code for corporate office employees. Retail store employees follow a specific dress code outlined below. The guiding principle is: dress appropriately for your role and the activities you will be engaging in during the workday.

## Corporate Office Dress Code

### Acceptable Attire

- Business casual: slacks, khakis, chinos
- Collared shirts, blouses, sweaters
- Denim (dark wash, no rips or distressing)
- Closed-toe shoes or clean, stylish sneakers
- Northwind Gear branded apparel (encouraged)

### Unacceptable Attire

- Beachwear, swimwear, or flip-flops
- Clothing with offensive language or imagery
- Athletic wear (unless exercising during lunch break)
- Torn, stained, or excessively worn clothing
- Hats or caps indoors (except for religious or medical reasons)

## Retail Store Dress Code

Store employees are expected to wear Northwind Gear branded clothing on shift:
- Northwind Gear branded polo, t-shirt, or sweater (provided by the company)
- Dark wash denim or black pants
- Clean, closed-toe shoes (sneakers approved)
- Name badge (provided by the store manager)

Employees receive 5 uniform pieces per year at no cost. Additional pieces are available at a 30% employee discount.

## Remote Work Dress Code

Remote employees are subject to the corporate office dress code for scheduled in-person meetings. For video calls, business-appropriate attire from the waist up is required. Casual attire is acceptable for audio-only meetings.

## Exceptions

Employees with medical conditions or religious obligations may request reasonable dress code accommodations. Requests should be submitted in writing to HR. Accommodations are reviewed on a case-by-case basis.
