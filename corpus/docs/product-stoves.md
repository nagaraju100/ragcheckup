## Troubleshooting Error Codes

| Code | Meaning | Solution |
|---|---|---|
| ERR-FL-01 | Fuel flow error | Check fuel canister connection |
| ERR-FL-02 | Low pressure warning | Prime fuel line, check fuel level |
| ERR-IGN-01 | Ignition failure | Check piezo connection or use match |
| ERR-IGN-02 | Igniter worn | Replace piezo igniter (PN: NW-ST-IGN) |
| ERR-FL-03 | Fuel valve stuck | Clean valve assembly, apply valve lubricant |

## Warranty

Canister stoves carry a 2-year warranty. Liquid fuel stoves carry a 5-year warranty (the most durable stove in our lineup). Integrated systems carry a 1-year warranty. All warranties exclude damage from abuse, foreign objects in the jet, or use of improper fuel.
