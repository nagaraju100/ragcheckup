# Employee Parking Policy

**Effective Date:** 2024-03-01
**Version:** 2024.1

## Overview

Northwind Gear provides parking for all employees who commute by vehicle. The corporate office in Boulder has 300 parking spaces. Retail store parking is subject to local municipal regulations.

## Corporate Office Parking

### Badge-Required Parking

All employees parking at the Boulder headquarters must display a valid Northwind Gear parking badge on their vehicle dashboard. Badges are issued upon hire and must be returned upon termination.

### Parking Rates

| Category | Monthly Rate | Allocation |
|---|---|---|
| Corporate employees (standard) | $50/month | First-come, first-served |
| Remote-first employees | $30/month | Limited availability |
| Retail managers (on-site rotations) | $50/month | Same as corporate |
| Visitors | Free | Visitor parking only |

### EV Charging

Northwind Gear has installed 20 EV charging stations in the south parking lot. EV charging is free for the first 2 hours per day. After 2 hours, a $1/hour fee applies. Employees must register their EV with Facilities at facilities@northwindgear.com.

## Reserved Spaces

Reserved parking spaces are available for:
- Employees with permanent disabilities (with documentation)
- Executive leadership (levels VP and above)
- VIP visitors (pre-requested through Corporate Relations)

## Bicycle Parking

Northwind Gear encourages cycling. The headquarters has:
- 50 bicycle parking spaces (covered racks)
- 4 shower facilities (gender-neutral)
- Basic repair tools and air pump
- Free bicycle commuter benefit: $50/month (pre-tax)

## Retail Store Parking

Store employees must follow local municipal parking regulations. If the store provides customer parking, employees must park in designated employee zones when available. No employee parking badges are required at retail locations.

## Violations

Vehicles parked without a valid badge, in reserved spaces, or in unauthorized areas will be:
- First violation: Warning sticker
- Second violation: Warning letter from Facilities
- Third violation: Towing at the owner's expense
