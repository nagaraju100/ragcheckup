# Northwind Gear Rewards Program

## Overview

The Northwind Gear Rewards program is our loyalty program for loyal customers. Earn points with every purchase and redeem them for exclusive rewards, early access to sales, and members-only events.

## How It Works

Enrollment is automatic upon your first purchase. You start earning points immediately:

- **Member:** 1 point per $1 spent
- **Silver:** 1.5 points per $1 spent (spend $500+ annually)
- **Gold:** 2 points per $1 spent (spend $1,500+ annually)
- **Platinum:** 2.5 points per $1 spent (spend $3,000+ annually)

## Point Expiration

Points expire 12 months after they are earned. Members receive an email reminder 30 days before expiration. Inactive accounts (no purchases or point activity for 24 months) will have remaining points forfeited.

## Redemption

Points can be redeemed for:
- Discount codes ($5 per 100 points)
- Exclusive product access (limited edition gear)
- Event tickets (store events, guided trips)
- Donation to the Northwind Foundation (50 points = $5 donation)

Redemption is done at checkout by selecting "Redeem Points" from the rewards dropdown. Points cannot be applied to shipping costs or taxes.

## Member Events

Gold and Platinum members are invited to:
- Annual gear showcase (September, Boulder HQ)
- Winter preview event (November, select stores)
- Summer preview event (May, select stores)
- Local store tasting events (quarterly, select stores)

Events are held at participating stores. Check your email for invitations.

## Program Changes

Northwind Gear reserves the right to modify the rewards program at any time. Changes will be communicated to members via email at least 30 days before taking effect. Existing points will honor the terms under which they were earned.
