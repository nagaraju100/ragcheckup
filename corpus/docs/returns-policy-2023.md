# Return & Refund Policy

**Effective Date:** 2023-01-15
**Version:** 2023.1

## Overview

Northwind Gear is committed to customer satisfaction. This policy covers all returns and refund requests for products purchased directly from Northwind Gear stores and the online shop. Items purchased through third-party retailers must be returned to the original retailer.

## Returns Eligibility

### Standard Returns

Products may be returned within **30 days** of the delivery date for a full refund to the original payment method. Products must be in their original packaging and unused. Items purchased on sale are eligible for return at the discounted price only.

### Extended Holiday Returns

Purchases made between November 1 and December 31 are eligible for returns through **January 31** of the following year. This extended window applies only to the current calendar year's holiday season.

### Final Sale Items

The following product categories are final sale and not eligible for return:
- Custom-engraved items
- Damaged goods reported after 48 hours of receipt
- Used camping gear (for hygiene reasons)

## Refund Processing

Refunds are processed within **5–7 business days** after the returned item is received and inspected. Customers will receive a confirmation email when the refund is issued. Processing times may vary depending on the customer's bank or credit card provider.

## Return Procedure

1. Contact customer support at support@northwindgear.com or call 1-800-555-0199.
2. Provide your order number and reason for return.
3. Receive a prepaid return shipping label via email.
4. Pack the item securely and ship within 14 days of receiving the label.

## Exchanges

Exchanges are available for size, color, or model swaps. The item must be returned and the replacement shipped as two separate transactions. Exchange shipping is free within the contiguous United States. International exchanges require the customer to cover return shipping costs.

## Damaged or Defective Items

Items received with manufacturing defects are covered under our **two-year warranty** (see Warranty Policy). Northwind Gear will cover all return shipping costs for verified manufacturing defects. Damage caused by normal use, abuse, or modification is not covered.
