# Leave & Time-Off Policy

**Effective Date:** 2023-02-01
**Version:** 2023.1

## Overview

Northwind Gear provides a competitive leave package to support employee well-being and work-life balance. This policy applies to all full-time employees hired before June 2023. Part-time employees receive prorated leave accruals.

## Paid Time Off (PTO)

### Accrual Rates

| Years of Service | Annual PTO Hours | Monthly Accrual | Carryover Limit |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–1 years | 120 hours | 10 hours | 40 hours |
| 2–4 years | 160 hours | 13.3 hours | 60 hours |
| 5–9 years | 200 hours | 16.7 hours | 80 hours |
| 10+ years | 240 hours | 20 hours | No limit |

Employees begin accruing PTO on their start date. New hires must use PTO after completing 90 days of employment. Unused PTO rolls over to the next calendar year up to the carryover limit.

## Sick Leave

Employees accrue **40 hours** of sick leave per year regardless of tenure. Sick leave may be used for personal illness, medical appointments, or to care for a family member. Documentation is required for absences exceeding 3 consecutive days.

### Sick Leave Usage

Sick leave requests should be submitted via the HR portal at least 24 hours in advance for planned appointments. Emergency sick leave may be reported by phone on the first day of absence.

## Paid Holidays

Northwind Gear observes **10 paid holidays** annually:
- New Year's Day (January 1)
- Martin Luther King Jr. Day (third Monday in January)
- Presidents' Day (third Monday in February)
- Memorial Day (last Monday in May)
- Independence Day (July 4)
- Labor Day (first Monday in September)
- Thanksgiving (fourth Thursday in November)
- Day after Thanksgiving
- Christmas Eve (half-day)
- Christmas Day (December 25)

Holiday employees who work on a scheduled holiday receive **time-and-a-half** pay. Retail store employees scheduled to work on a holiday receive **double time**.

## Parental Leave

### Maternity Leave
Full-time female employees are entitled to **12 weeks** of paid maternity leave following childbirth. The leave must be taken within the first 12 months after the birth or adoption. A medical certificate is required for leave exceeding 4 weeks.

### Paternal Leave
All full-time employees are entitled to **4 weeks** of paid paternal leave following the birth or adoption of a child. This leave must be taken within 6 months of the child's placement.

### Adoption Leave
Employees adopting a child through a licensed agency are entitled to **8 weeks** of paid leave. The leave must begin within 30 days of the child's placement.

## Unpaid Leave

Employees with at least 12 months of service may request up to **8 weeks** of unpaid leave for medical, family, or personal reasons. Requests must be submitted in writing at least 30 days in advance, except for emergency situations.
