# Recruitment Guide for Hiring Managers

**Effective Date:** 2024-04-01
**Version:** 2024.1

## Overview

This guide walks hiring managers through Northwind Gear's recruitment process, from requisition to onboarding. Please follow all steps to ensure a fair and consistent hiring experience.

## Step 1: Requisition

All new hires require a completed Job Requisition Form (JRF-2024) submitted to HR at least 30 days before the desired start date. The JRF must include:
- Position title and department
- Justification (new role or backfill)
- Required qualifications and nice-to-haves
- Salary range (based on current salary band)
- Start date requested
- Hiring manager and recruiter assignments

## Step 2: Sourcing

Approved requisitions are posted on:
- Northwind Gear careers page (northwindgear.com/careers)
- Indeed, LinkedIn, and ZipRecruiter (approved channels only)
- Internal job board (for current employees applying externally)

Diversity sourcing is encouraged through partnerships with:
- Outdoors women organizations
- Veteran job boards
- Local community college career centers

## Step 3: Screening

All applications are reviewed within 5 business days. Candidates who meet minimum qualifications receive an initial phone screen (30 minutes) from the recruiter. The recruiter uses the standardized screening form (SRC-2024).

## Step 4: Interview Process

| Stage | Description | Duration | Panel |
|---|---|---|---|
| Phone Screen | Recruiter assessment | 30 min | Recruiter |
| Technical Assessment | Role-specific skills test | 60 min | Hiring Manager |
| Panel Interview | Cultural fit + cross-functional | 90 min | 3–4 panelists |
| Final Interview | Department head + HR | 45 min | Dept Head, HR |
| Reference Checks | 3 professional references | N/A | HR |

## Step 5: Offer

HR prepares the offer letter based on the approved salary band. Offer letters include:
- Start date
- Compensation and bonus structure
- Benefits enrollment information
- Contingencies (background check, drug screening)

Candidates have 5 business days to accept or decline an offer.

## Step 6: Onboarding

Successful new hires receive:
- Welcome package (Northwind Gear apparel, swag)
- IT equipment (laptop, badge, phone)
- HR orientation (day 1)
- Department-specific training (weeks 1–4)
- Mentor assignment (week 1)
