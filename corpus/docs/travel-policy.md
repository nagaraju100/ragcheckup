# Business Travel Policy

**Effective Date:** 2024-03-15
**Version:** 2024.1

## Overview

Northwind Gear encourages business travel as essential to our operations. This policy covers travel arrangements, per diem allowances, booking procedures, and expense reimbursement for employees traveling on company business.

## Approval Requirements

All business travel requires pre-approval from the employee's direct manager. Travel exceeding $2,000 (total estimated cost) requires additional VP-level approval. Emergency travel may be approved verbally, with written confirmation within 48 hours.

## Booking Procedures

Employees must book travel through the corporate travel portal (travel.northwindgear.com). Preferred vendors include:
- Airlines: United Airlines (corporate rate agreement)
- Hotels: Marriott, Hilton (corporate negotiated rates)
- Car rentals: Enterprise, Hertz (corporate accounts)

Exceptions to using preferred vendors require written justification and VP approval.

## Flight Class

- Employees with less than 3 years tenure: Economy class only
- Employees with 3–7 years tenure: Economy or premium economy (up to $300 fare increase)
- Employees with 7+ years tenure: Business class for flights exceeding 4 hours one-way

## Hotel Standards

Employees should book hotels at or below the corporate negotiated rate for the area. When negotiated rates are unavailable, the employee may book at the prevailing commercial rate with manager approval. Maximum hotel per night rates by city category:
- Tier 1 cities (NYC, SF, LA, Chicago): $250/night
- Tier 2 cities: $180/night
- Tier 3 cities: $140/night

## Ground Transportation

Ground transportation (taxi, rideshare, rental car) is fully reimbursable when booked through the corporate portal. Employees who choose to use personal vehicles for business travel will receive mileage reimbursement at the current GSA rate (67 cents per mile for 2024).

## Travel Insurance

Northwind Gear provides trip cancellation and interruption insurance for all company-booked travel. Employees are encouraged to review the policy details at insurance.northwindgear.com before booking international travel.

## Emergency Contacts

During international travel, employees must register their itinerary at travel.northwindgear.com/emergency. The Global Assistance hotline (1-800-555-0200) provides 24/7 support for medical emergencies, legal assistance, and travel disruption assistance.

## Post-Trip Requirements

Employees must submit expense reports within 14 days of trip completion. Unsubmitted expense reports older than 30 days will be automatically rejected and re-booked as a personal loan against the employee's next paycheck.
