# Frequently Asked Questions

## General Questions

### What is Northwind Gear's return policy?
Northwind Gear offers a 30-day return window for most products (45 days for 2025 policy). Products must be in original packaging. Final sale items cannot be returned. For details, see our Returns & Refund Policy.

### How do I track my order?
All shipping confirmations include a tracking number. You can track your order at northwindgear.com/track or through the Northwind Gear app under "My Orders."

### Do you offer international shipping?
Yes, Northwind Gear ships to over 40 countries. International orders may be subject to customs duties and taxes, which are the responsibility of the recipient. Shipping times vary from 5–21 business days depending on destination.

## Product Questions

### Which tent is best for backpacking?
For solo backpackers under 70kg, we recommend the NW-TR-2A Summit Explorer 2P (1.4 kg). For couples or gear-heavy trips, the NW-TR-4A Family Explorer 4P offers more space. For extreme conditions, the NW-TR-4B Expedition Lodge 4P is our most durable option.

### What sleeping bag temperature rating do I need?
- Summer camping (above 10°C / 50°F): NW-SB-SL Summer (rated to 10°C)
- Spring/Fall camping (0°C / 32°F and above): NW-SB-3S Three-Season (rated to 0°C)
- Winter camping (below 0°C): NW-SB-W1 Winter (rated to -20°C)

### Do you sell replacement parts?
Yes. All tent, stove, and backpack parts are available individually. See each product page for the replacement parts list, or contact our parts department at parts@northwindgear.com.

## Account & Orders

### How do I create an account?
Create an account at northwindgear.com/register using your email address. Existing guests can upgrade their order confirmation email to a full account.

### Can I modify my order after checkout?
Orders can be modified within 2 hours of placement. After 2 hours, the order moves to our warehouse for processing. Contact customer support at 1-800-555-0199 for assistance.

## Warranty

### How does the warranty work?
Northwind Gear offers warranties ranging from 1 to 5 years depending on the product category. All warranties cover manufacturing defects under normal use. See the individual product pages for specific warranty terms.

### How do I file a warranty claim?
Email warranty@northwindgear.com with photos of the issue, your purchase receipt, and the product SKU. Claims are processed within 5–10 business days.
