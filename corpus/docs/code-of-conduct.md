# Code of Conduct

**Effective Date:** 2024-01-15
**Version:** 2024.1

## Core Principles

Northwind Gear expects all employees, contractors, and partners to uphold the highest standards of professional conduct. This code applies to all business activities, whether on-site, remote, or at company events.

## Respectful Workplace

All employees are entitled to work in an environment free from harassment, discrimination, and intimidation. This includes protection based on race, gender, age, religion, disability, sexual orientation, or any other characteristic protected by applicable law.

## Anti-Harassment Policy

Harassment includes but is not limited to:
- Offensive jokes or comments
- Physical contact or threats
- Displays of offensive materials
- Unwelcome sexual advances
- Retaliation against those who report concerns

Employees who experience or witness harassment should report it immediately to HR or their manager. All complaints are treated confidentially and investigated within 10 business days.

## Conflict of Interest

Employees must disclose any outside employment, business interests, or personal relationships that could reasonably be perceived as a conflict with Northwind Gear's interests. Conflict disclosures must be updated annually and whenever a new conflict arises.

## IT and Data Security

All employees must comply with the IT Security Policy. This includes:
- Using strong passwords (minimum 12 characters)
- Enabling two-factor authentication on all company systems
- Reporting lost or stolen devices within 1 hour
- Not sharing credentials under any circumstances
- Keeping all software updated to the latest stable version

Employees who violate IT security policies may face disciplinary action, up to and including termination. Security incidents must be reported to the IT helpdesk at helpdesk@northwindgear.com or extension 5500.

## Social Media Guidelines

Employees may share personal opinions on social media, provided they:
- Clearly state that views are their own and not Northwind Gear's
- Do not share confidential company information
- Do not represent the company when speaking publicly without authorization
- Comply with applicable advertising and competition laws

Productivity Monitoring

Northwind Gear monitors internet usage during work hours to ensure compliance with acceptable use policies. Automated monitoring tools track bandwidth usage and access to unauthorized websites. Employees should expect that company-issued devices and network traffic are subject to monitoring.

Customer Feedback System

The company values customer input and monitors feedback across all channels. Customer complaints received via social media, email, or phone are logged in the CRM system and routed to the appropriate department within 4 business hours.

Training Requirements

All employees must complete annual compliance training covering harassment prevention, data security, and code of conduct updates. Completion must be submitted by March 31 each year. Departments with higher-risk functions (finance, IT, HR) must complete quarterly refresher training.

## Reporting Concerns

Employees who have concerns about workplace conduct may report them through:
- Direct manager (preferred for minor issues)
- HR department: hr@northwindgear.com or 1-800-555-0155
- Anonymous hotline: 1-800-555-0199 (available 24/7)
- Ethics committee (for issues involving senior management)

All reports are reviewed within 5 business days. Retaliation against reporters is strictly prohibited and will result in disciplinary action.
