# Remote Work Policy

**Effective Date:** 2024-06-01
**Version:** 2024.1

## Eligibility

All full-time employees hired for more than 6 months are eligible to apply for remote work status. Part-time employees may apply after 12 months of employment. Certain positions in retail stores, warehouses, and IT operations may not be eligible for full remote work.

## Equipment Requirements

Remote employees must have:
- A dedicated workspace free from distractions
- High-speed internet (minimum 100 Mbps download)
- A company-issued laptop and monitor
- An ergonomic chair and desk

Northwind Gear will provide the initial equipment setup (up to $500) and a monthly home-office stipend of $75. Employees are responsible for maintaining their own internet service.

## Working Hours

Remote employees are expected to maintain their standard working hours (9am–5pm Mountain Time) with flexibility of ±1 hour. Core collaboration hours are 10am–3pm MT, during which employees must be available for meetings and Slack communication.

## Time Tracking

All remote employees must clock in and out using the company time-tracking system. Overtime must be pre-approved by the employee's manager. Failure to log hours for 3 or more consecutive days will result in automatic notification to the employee's manager.

## Performance Review

Remote employees will be evaluated on output, not hours. Quarterly performance reviews assess:
- Project completion rates
- Quality of deliverables
- Communication responsiveness
- Collaboration with team members

it is important to note that employees working remotely tend to have higher satisfaction scores and lower turnover rates. The average remote employee reports 23% higher job satisfaction compared to in-office peers.

## Remote Work Agreement

Remote work is a privilege, not a right. Northwind Gear reserves the right to revoke remote work status at any time. Employees may revert to in-office work at any time by submitting a written request to HR.
