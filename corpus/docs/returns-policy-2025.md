# Return & Refund Policy

**Effective Date:** 2025-03-01
**Version:** 2025.1

## Overview

Northwind Gear is committed to customer satisfaction. This policy covers all returns and refund requests for products purchased directly from Northwind Gear stores and the online shop. Items purchased through third-party retailers must be returned to the original retailer.

## Returns Eligibility

### Standard Returns

Products may be returned within **45 days** of the delivery date for a full refund to the original payment method. Products must be in their original packaging and unused. Items purchased on sale are eligible for return at the discounted price only.

### Extended Holiday Returns

Purchases made between November 1 and December 31 are eligible for returns through **February 28** of the following year. This extended window applies only to the current calendar year's holiday season.

### Final Sale Items

The following product categories are final sale and not eligible for return:
- Custom-engraved items
- Used camping gear (for hygiene reasons)
- Open-box items without original packaging
- Gift cards

## Refund Processing

Refunds are processed within **3–5 business days** after the returned item is received and inspected. Customers will receive a confirmation email when the refund is issued. Processing times may vary depending on the customer's bank or credit card provider. Express refunds (same day) are available for Gold loyalty members.

## Returns Procedure

1. Contact customer support via the Northwind Gear app (preferred method), email, or phone.
2. Provide your order number and reason for return.
3. Receive a prepaid return shipping label via email or the app.
4. Pack the item securely and ship within 21 days of receiving the label.

## Exchanges

Exchanges are available for size, color, or model swaps. Gold and Platinum loyalty members receive free two-way shipping on all exchanges. Standard members receive free return shipping only. The replacement item ships immediately upon scan of the returned item at our warehouse.

## Damaged or Defective Items

Items received with manufacturing defects are covered under our **three-year warranty** (see Warranty Policy). Northwind Gear will cover all return shipping costs for verified manufacturing defects. Damage caused by normal use, abuse, or modification is not covered. A photo inspection may be required for items over $200.
