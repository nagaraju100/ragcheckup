# Product Warranty Policy

**Effective Date:** 2024-01-01
**Version:** 2024.1

## Overview

Northwind Gear stands behind the quality of every product we sell. This warranty covers manufacturing defects in materials and workmanship for the periods specified for each product category.

## Warranty Periods

| Product Category | Warranty Period | What's Covered |
|---|---|---|
| Tents | 2 years | Fabric tears (not abuse), zipper failures, pole breakage (normal use), seam separation |
| Backpacks | 5 years | Zipper failures, buckle breakage, fabric delamination, frame damage |
| Stoves | 2 years (canister), 5 years (liquid fuel) | Igniter failure, fuel flow issues, seal deterioration, burner clogging (normal use) |
| Sleeping bags | 2 years | Zipper failures, baffles coming apart, loss of insulation (manufacturing defect) |
| Clothing | 1 year | Seam separation, zipper failures, fabric defects (not normal wear) |
| Accessories | 2 years | Manufacturing defects (batteries not covered) |
| Boats | 3 years (inflatable), 5 years (kayak/canoe) | Seam separation, valve failures, hull cracks (normal use) |

## What's NOT Covered

- Damage from normal wear and tear
- Damage from abuse, neglect, or improper storage
- Damage from modification or unauthorized repair
- Cosmetic damage that does not affect product performance
- Batteries (any type)
- Normal fading from sun exposure
- Damage from improper use (e.g., using a tent as a ground cloth)

## How to Claim

To file a warranty claim:
1. Email warranty@northwindgear.com
2. Include photos showing the issue
3. Include your purchase receipt or order number
4. Include the product SKU (found on the product label)

Claims are processed within 5–10 business days. Approved claims receive a repair, replacement, or store credit at Northwind Gear's discretion.

## Exclusions

- Sale items are covered under the same warranty as full-price items
- Gift cards are not covered under warranty
- Products purchased from unauthorized resellers are not covered
- International purchases may have different warranty terms

## Limitations

This warranty is in addition to your statutory rights. Northwind Gear's liability is limited to the purchase price of the product. No consequential or incidental damages are covered.
