# Emergency Procedures

**Effective Date:** 2024-01-15
**Version:** 2024.1

## Building Evacuation

In the event of a fire alarm, earthquake, or other building emergency, all employees must:
1. Remain calm and follow posted evacuation routes
2. Exit the building via the nearest marked exit
3. Assemble at the designated staging area (Flatirons Boulevard parking lot)
4. Do not re-enter the building until cleared by building management

Evacuation wardens (identified by orange vests) are responsible for checking each floor before evacuating.

## Fire Safety

- Fire extinguishers are located on every floor near stairwells
- Pull stations are mounted near all exits
- Employees must NOT use elevators during a fire evacuation
- Assembly area: Flatirons Blvd parking lot, north end

## Severe Weather

### Thunderstorms
Seek shelter in interior rooms away from windows. Do not use landline phones.

### Earthquakes
Duck, cover, and hold. Get under a sturdy desk or against an interior wall. Protect your head and neck. Do not run outside during shaking.

### Winter Storms
Corporate offices may close due to severe winter weather. Closure announcements are made by 5:30 AM MT via:
- Company-wide email
- Text alert (opt-in)
- northwindgear.com status page
- Local news stations

Employees working remotely during a closure may work from home or use a personal day.

## Medical Emergencies

Call 911 for any medical emergency. Northwind Gear's building address is 1200 Flatirons Boulevard, Boulder, CO 80302.

AED units are located:
- First floor, east lobby
- Second floor, near the cafeteria
- Basement level, gym entrance

First-aid kits are available on each floor. Employees trained in CPR/AED are listed on the emergency contacts board (lobbies) and the intranet.

## Active Threat

If you encounter an active threat:
1. **Run** — Escape if a safe route is available
2. **Hide** — If escape is not possible, find a secure location, silence your phone, and stay out of sight
3. **Fight** — As a last resort, attempt to incapacitate the threat

Security code for the building: **NWG-SEC-2024**. Share this code only with building management and security personnel.

All incidents (injuries, near-misses, property damage) must be reported to the Safety Officer within 24 hours. Incident reports are available on the intranet (DOC-SAF-2024-001).
