
# Shipping Guidelines

**Effective Date:** 2024-03-01
**Version:** 2024.1

## Domestic Shipping (United States)

### Standard Shipping

Orders ship within 1–2 business days. Delivery time is 3–7 business days depending on destination.

| Order Total | Shipping Cost | Estimated Delivery |
|---|---|---|
| Under $50 | $7.99 | 5–7 business days |
| $50–$99.99 | $5.99 | 4–6 business days |
| $100+ | FREE | 3–5 business days |

### Express Shipping

Available for orders shipped to the contiguous United States. Delivery in 1–2 business days.

| Order Total | Shipping Cost | Estimated Delivery |
|---|---|---|
| Under $50 | $14.99 | 1–2 business days |
| $50+ | $9.99 | 1–2 business days |

### Overnight Shipping

Available for orders placed before 2pm MT. Delivery next business day by 10:30am.

| Order Total | Shipping Cost |
|---|---|
| All orders | $24.99 |

## International Shipping

Northwind Gear ships to over 40 countries. International shipping costs are calculated at checkout based on weight, dimensions, and destination.

| Region | Standard (7–21 days) | Express (3–7 days) |
|---|---|---|
| Canada | $12.99 | $24.99 |
| Europe | $19.99 | $39.99 |
| Australia / NZ | $24.99 | $49.99 |
| Asia-Pacific | $24.99 | $49.99 |
| South America | $29.99 | $59.99 |

International orders may be subject to customs duties and taxes, which are the responsibility of the recipient.

## Packaging

All orders are shipped in Northwind Gear branded packaging, which is 100% recyclable. Eco-friendly packaging uses 40% less material than standard packaging. Custom gift wrapping is available for an additional $4.99 per order.

## Tracking

All shipped orders include a tracking number via email. Tracking updates are available at northwindgear.com/track.

## Returns & Exchanges

Items may be returned within 30 days (45 days for 2025 policy) of delivery. The customer is responsible for return shipping costs unless the item is defective.
