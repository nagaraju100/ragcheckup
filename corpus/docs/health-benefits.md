# Employee Health Benefits Guide

**Effective Date:** 2024-01-01
**Version:** 2024.1

## Overview

Northwind Gear provides comprehensive health benefits to all full-time employees, with optional add-ons for part-time employees who work 30+ hours per week. Benefits coverage begins on the first day of employment.

## Medical Insurance

### Plans Available

| Plan | Monthly Premium (Employee) | Deductible | Out-of-Pocket Max | Network |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| HMO Basic | $185 | $2,500 | $5,000 | Select |
| PPO Standard | $320 | $1,500 | $6,000 | Broad |
| PPO Premium | $485 | $500 | $4,000 | Broadest |

Northwind Gear covers 80% of the premium for employee-only coverage and 60% for family coverage.

### Prescription Drug Coverage

All three plans include prescription drug coverage with the following tiers:
- Tier 1 (generic): $10 copay
- Tier 2 (brand): $35 copay
- Tier 3 (specialty): 20% coinsurance

### Dental & Vision

| Service | HMO Basic | PPO Standard | PPO Premium |
|---|---|---|---|
| Preventive dental (2x/year) | 100% | 100% | 100% |
| Basic dental (fillings) | 80% | 80% | 80% |
| Major dental (crowns) | 50% | 50% | 50% |
| Vision exam | 100% | 100% | 100% |
| Frames/lenses allowance | $150/yr | $200/yr | $250/yr |
| Contact lenses | $100/yr | $150/yr | $200/yr |

## Retirement

### 401(k) Plan
Employees are eligible to participate in the 401(k) plan on their first day. Northwind Gear matches 100% of employee contributions up to 6% of salary. vesting schedule: 25% per year, fully vested after 4 years.

### Health Savings Account (HSA)
Available with the HMO Basic plan. Northwind Gear contributes $500/year to employee HSA accounts. Contributions are made per pay period.

## Additional Benefits

- Life insurance: 1x annual salary (company-paid)
- Disability insurance: 60% of salary after 90-day waiting period
- Employee Assistance Program (EAP): Free confidential counseling
- Flexible spending account (FSA): Up to $3,000/year
- Pet insurance discount: 15% off through Northwind's partner
