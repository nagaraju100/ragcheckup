# Annual Training Calendar 2024

## Mandatory Training

All Northwind Gear employees must complete the following training annually:

| Course | Deadline | Format | Duration | Credit |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Code of Conduct Refresher | March 31 | Online | 2 hours | Required |
| Data Security Awareness | March 31 | Online | 1.5 hours | Required |
| Anti-Harassment Prevention | June 30 | In-person (select cities) | 3 hours | Required |
| Fire Safety & Evacuation | June 30 | In-person (select cities) | 1 hour | Required |
| IT Access Control | March 31 | Online | 1 hour | Required |

## Optional Training

| Course | Dates | Format | Duration | Credit |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Leadership Development | Monthly | In-person (HQ) | 8 hours/day (2 days) | Optional |
| Technical Skills Workshop | Quarterly | In-person (select cities) | 4 hours | Optional |
| First Aid & CPR Certification | Quarterly | In-person (HQ) | 8 hours | Optional |
| Product Knowledge Seminar | Quarterly | Online | 3 hours | Optional |
| Customer Service Excellence | Bi-annually | In-person (select cities) | 6 hours | Optional |

## Department-Specific Training

### Retail Store Employees
- New Hire Orientation: Weeks 1–2 (in-person)
- Point of Sale System Training: Week 3 (in-person)
- Product Knowledge: Monthly (online)
- Loss Prevention: Annual (online)
- Visual Merchandising: Bi-annually (in-person)

### Corporate Employees
- Software Training: As needed (online)
- Compliance Training: Annual (online)
- Diversity & Inclusion: Annual (online)
- Project Management: Optional (in-person or online)

### Warehouse Employees
- Forklift Certification: Before operating equipment
- Safety Protocol: Quarterly (in-person)
- Inventory Management: Annual (online)
- Manual Handling: Annual (in-person)

## Training Requests

Employees may request additional training by completing a Training Request Form (TRF-2024) and submitting it to their manager. Approved requests may be reimbursed up to $500 per year for external courses, seminars, or certifications.

## Tracking

Training completions are tracked in the Learning Management System (LMS) at lms.northwindgear.com. Department managers are responsible for ensuring their team members complete mandatory training by the deadlines listed above.
