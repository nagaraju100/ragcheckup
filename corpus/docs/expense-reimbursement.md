# Employee Expense Reimbursement Policy

**Effective Date:** 2024-02-01
**Version:** 2024.1

## Purpose

This policy governs the submission and reimbursement of business-related expenses incurred by Northwind Gear employees. All expenses must be necessary, reasonable, and directly related to company business.

## Submission Guidelines

Expenses must be submitted through the corporate expense portal (expenses.northwindgear.com) within **30 days** of incurring the expense. Late submissions may be rejected at the discretion of the Finance team.

## Categories

### Meals & Entertainment

| Meal Type | Per Person Limit | Receipt Required |
|---|---|---|
| Standard business lunch | $35 | Yes, over $25 |
| Standard business dinner | $65 | Yes, over $50 |
| Client entertainment | $75 | Yes, always |
| Team building events | $50 | Yes, always |
| Coffee/refreshments | $15 | No, under $25 |
| Travel day meals | $55 | Yes, over $50 |

Expenses exceeding the per-person limit require pre-approval from the employee's manager and Finance.

### Travel Expenses

| Airfare (economy) | Actual cost | Actual cost | Corporate negotiated rates |
| Ground transportation | Actual cost | Actual cost | Tax/rideshare/subway |
| Hotel (Tier 1) | $250/night | $350/night | Per Travel Policy |
| Hotel (Tier 2) | $180/night | $250/night | Per Travel Policy |

| Baggage fees | $75 first bag | $150 second bag | Per bag maximum |

### Office Supplies

Standard office supplies (paper, pens, notepads) under $50 per month require no approval. Requests above $50 must be submitted through the Procurement department. Equipment purchases follow the Capital Expenditure policy.

### Client Gifts

Client gifts are limited to $50 per recipient per year, with a maximum of 10 recipients per employee annually. All gifts must be documented with the recipient's name, company, and business purpose. Gift cards are prohibited.

### Technology & Equipment

Employees requesting technology equipment must submit a Business Case Form (BCF-2024) with:
- Description of equipment
- Justification for business use
- Estimated cost
- Alternative solutions considered
- Manager approval signature

Standard equipment packages are provided at no cost. Upgrades beyond the standard package require VP approval.

## Mileage Reimbursement

Employees using personal vehicles for business travel are reimbursed at **67 cents per mile** (2024 rate). Mileage logs must include:
- Date of travel
- Starting and ending locations
- Business purpose
- Total miles

Monthly mileage reimbursement requests must include a map screenshot from a route-planning service.

## Reimbursement Timeline

Approved expenses are reimbursed within **10 business days** via direct deposit. International reimbursements may take up to 20 business days. Employees may receive advance payments of up to $500 for anticipated expenses with Finance approval.

## Audit & Compliance

Random audits of expense reports are conducted quarterly. Duplicate submissions, inflated expenses, or personal expenses claimed as business expenses may result in reimbursement denial and disciplinary action.
