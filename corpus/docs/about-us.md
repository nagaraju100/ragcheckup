# About Northwind Gear

## Our Story

Northwind Gear was founded in 2010 in Boulder, Colorado, by outdoor enthusiasts Mark and Sarah Chen. What began as a small gear shop at the base of Flatirons Boulevard has grown into a national retailer with 23 stores and a thriving e-commerce platform.

## Mission

We believe that the great outdoors is the best classroom, the best therapist, and the best adventure. Our mission is to equip people of all experience levels with reliable, thoughtfully designed gear that performs when it matters most.

## Values

**Quality Over Quantity:** Every product in our catalog meets our rigorous standards. We test each item in real field conditions before adding it to our inventory.

**Sustainability:** We are committed to reducing our environmental footprint. Since 2022, we have sourced 60% of our materials from recycled or sustainably harvested sources. By 2027, we aim for 100%.

**Community:** We give back through the Northwind Foundation, which has funded 45 local trail maintenance projects and partnered with Outdoor Alliance to place 12,000 young people in outdoor programs.

**Expertise:** Our staff are outdoor enthusiasts who use our products themselves. Every store employee completes 40 hours of field training before their first customer interaction.

## Leadership

### Mark Chen — Co-Founder & CEO
Mark's passion for alpine climbing drives Northwind's product selection. He has summited 14 peaks above 6,000 meters and personally tests all tent and sleeping bag products.

### Sarah Chen — Co-Founder & COO
Sarah manages operations, supply chain, and retail strategy. Before Northwind, she spent 8 years in retail management for a national sporting goods chain.

### Dr. Patricia Rivera — VP of Product
A materials scientist with 15 years in outdoor gear R&D, Dr. Rivera leads our product development team and oversees quality testing.

## Store Locations

We operate 23 stores across 14 states and 2 Canadian provinces, from Seattle to Boston and from Calgary to Austin. Each store features a product demonstration area, a repair workshop, and a community bulletin board for local outdoor events.
