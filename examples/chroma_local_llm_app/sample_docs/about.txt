Our company was founded in 2020 by three engineers who met at an IIT alumni meetup in Bengaluru. They shared a vision of building AI tools that make complex data accessible to non-technical businesses.

The company started in a small co-working space in Whitefield with just two laptops and a whiteboard. Within the first year, they grew to 50 employees and moved to a dedicated office space.

Today, NexusAI serves over 200 enterprise clients across healthcare, finance, and retail sectors in India, Singapore, and the United States.
